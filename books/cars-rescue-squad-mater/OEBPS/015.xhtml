The factory blew up in a huge explosion!

Luckily, by then Lightning was out of the building.
 Rescue Squad Mater had done it. He’d saved the day.

“Yay, Mater!” the onlookers cheered. “Awesome!”

Mater didn’t have time to celebrate. He had to make
 sure his best friend wasn’t hurt. He used his ladder to lift
Lightning into an ambulance.

“The hospital, stat!”  he ordered.
 “He’s overheating.”

“I got it!” the ambulance said, and then sped away.

Finally Mater turned toward the crowd and smiled.
