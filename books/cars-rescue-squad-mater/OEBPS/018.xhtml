Seconds later, the doctor rolled in. Lightning
 could hardly believe his eyes. It was Mater! He
 looked calm and professional in his surgical
mask and headlamp.

“All righty, now,” Dr. Mater told his patient.

“Let’s have a look-see.”

“Mater, you’re a doctor, too?” Lightning
asked in surprise.

“That’s right, buddy,” Dr. Mater replied. “Got
 my MD, my PhD, my STP, and my GTO.”

Lightning spotted Dr. Mater’s diplomas on
the wall. What kind of a degree was a GTO? he
 wondered.
