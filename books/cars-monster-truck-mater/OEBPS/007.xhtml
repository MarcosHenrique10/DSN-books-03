The announcer ignored him. Instead, he
 introduced the Tormentor’s opponent.
 “The I-Screamer!”

A clown-faced ice-cream truck with monster
 wheels rolled into the ring.

The Tormentor wasn’t sure how to wrestle
 such a big truck. So he quickly put on a beanie
 cap, hoping to trick his opponent instead. “Can
 I have one double-dip dipstick sundae, please?”

“Huh?” said the I-Screamer. “Oh, sure.”

When the ice-cream truck reached for a
 sundae, the Tormentor grabbed the wrestler’s
 bumper with his tow hook and flipped him.
 Then the Tormentor sprayed the truck with a
 fire extinguisher. Now the I-Screamer looked
 like an ice-cream sundae!

The referee announced to the crowd that
 the Tormentor had won.
