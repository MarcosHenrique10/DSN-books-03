“The winners!” the referee announced.
 “The Tormentor and . . .” He turned to Lightning. “What’s
 your name, son?”

“Lightning McQueen,” he replied.

“And Frightening McMean!” the ref declared.

Outside Flo’s V8 Café, Lightning listened doubtfully to
 the end of Mater’s story. Just then a fan rolled up and
 asked for the Tormentor’s autograph.

“Don’t you want Frightening McMean’s autograph?”
 Lightning asked.

“No, thanks,” the fan replied and drove away.
