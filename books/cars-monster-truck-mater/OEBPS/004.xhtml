One day outside Flo’s V8 Café, Lightning McQueen
 pointed out a monster truck that was driving by.

“I used to wrestle trucks bigger than that,” Mater told
 him. “I used to be a monster-truck wrestler!”

Mater began to tell Lightning a story about the time
 he was a wrestler called the Tormentor. His first match
 was in a dark arena filled with cheering fans. Mater wore
 a blue-and-red wrestling mask.
