After his first win, the Tormentor got a new paint job.

Soon he faced Captain Collision, a military truck.
 “Drop and give me twenty!” Captain Collision shouted.

Instead the Tormentor bounced off the ropes and
 knocked the captain right onto his back.

“One . . . two . . . three,”
 the referee counted. Then he declared a winner: the
 Tormentor!
