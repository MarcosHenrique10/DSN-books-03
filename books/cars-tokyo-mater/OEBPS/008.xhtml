Then Mater rolled backward and accidentally bumped into
 Kabuto, the leader of a gang of ninja cars.

“You scratched my paint,” Kabuto snarled. He circled
 around Mater, his tires kicking up a big cloud of smoke. “Dorifuto de shoubu da!” he said
 in Japanese.

“What-sa?” asked Mater.

“He challenges you to a drift race.” Ito-san explained that
 in a drift race, a car drives fast and steers hard into turns.
 That type of driving makes the car slide on the road.

“We will race at midnight,” Kabuto said, then sped away.
