“Ready . . . set . . . go!”

Kabuto and Mater peeled out, zipping through the
 streets. Kabuto steered around sharp corners and drifted
 at high speeds. He was in the lead! Mater tried to catch up.
 But he was driving so fast that he missed a turn.

“You can’t drift! Ha-ha-ha!” Kabuto laughed.

“I’ll show you!” Mater declared.
