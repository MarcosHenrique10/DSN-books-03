Mater described to Lightning how he was driving through
 Carburetor County and saw an older car sitting on the side of
 the road. Mater pulled up.

“Looks like you could use a tow somewhere,” he said.

“It is very far,” replied the older car. His name was Ito-san.

“Well, no tow is too far for Tow Mater!” exclaimed Mater.
