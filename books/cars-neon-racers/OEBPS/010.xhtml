The flag dropped and the racers took off! Francesco and
most of the other racers headed down the mountain. But Lightning
wasn’t about to take the easy way out. He wanted the honor
of signing the climbers’ book!

Lightning, Shu, Carla, Lewis, and Vitaly Petrov, the Russian racer,
drove up the rugged terrain, hugging the curves and skidding
across patches of ice on their way to the summit, their neon
lighting the way.
