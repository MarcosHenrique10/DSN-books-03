At his headquarters, Shu explained the race. “We’ll be racing at
night— an 85- mile trip from Mount Fuji to Ginza.”

Shu motioned for several of his pit crew members to come
forward. “Since we’ll be racing in the dark, we’ll all need to be
outfitted with special competition lighting.” Shu darkened
his race shop. The other racers gasped. Shu was glowing!
