The next evening, the racers— all lit up in bright neon— met
at Base Camp 5, halfway up Mount Fuji.

“Welcome, racers,” said Shu. “When the flag drops, you may head
straight down the mountain. But, for those who would like to
join the ranks of Mount Fuji’s most legendary racers, you
may head up . . . and sign the climbers’ book at the top of the
mountain!”
