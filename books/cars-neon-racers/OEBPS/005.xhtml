McQueen was competing in the
Transcontinental Race of Champions— T-ROC— a
series of races hosted by his international racing friends. Now
Lightning was in Japan for a race hosted by Shu Todoroki.

“Then I’m afraid Francesco will be very disappointed . . . especially
