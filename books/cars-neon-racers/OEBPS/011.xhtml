When the racers reached the summit, Shu called out, “There it
is! The climbers’ book!”

“Ka- chow!” Lightning cried, signing the book. The other racers
followed suit.

As the racers turned to head down the mountain, Shu stopped
them. “I forgot to mention the other reward for reaching the peak
of Mount Fuji: there’s another road that leads straight
down! Follow me!”

“Now you’re talkin’!” yelled Lightning.
