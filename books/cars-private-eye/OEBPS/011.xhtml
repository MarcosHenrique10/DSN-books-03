Mater wasn’t going to give up. Luckily, a friendly garbage
truck gave Mater a clue that led him to the docks.

Then he saw Mia on the deck of a huge cargo boat! She
was wearing a cement boot to prevent her from escaping.

Mater hid inside a crate and tried to sneak onto the
boat to rescue her.

At that moment, the crate lifted up. Mater had been
spotted!
