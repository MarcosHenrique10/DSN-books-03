A car named Tia drove in. She was wearing a black veil and
whitewall tires. “I need you to find my sister, Mia,” cried Tia.
“She’s been carnapped!”

“Where did you last see her?” asked Mater.

“She was working for Big D at his club,” said Tia. “The
Carbacabana.”

Big D was a fancy sedan who had recently opened a new
nightclub.
