At last the results were in! Incredibly, all three cars had

finished at once! A tiebreaker race would be held in California
in a week.

“Hey, rook! First one to California gets Dinoco all to

himself,” taunted Chick.

McQueen had a lot to learn, but for now, as he left

the Winners’ Circle, he thought of one thing: getting to
California—and fast. . . . Ka-chow!
