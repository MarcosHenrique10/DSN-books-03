At the end of the evening, Lightning told Mater he wanted
to head to the Black Forest to practice on the racetrack.

An old gentlecar overheard their plans. “Black Forest at
night, eh? Just beware of that Waldgeister Monster. It’s
the fastest and scariest monster in the forest.”

“A m...m...monster?” said Mater.

The old car chuckled and drove off.
