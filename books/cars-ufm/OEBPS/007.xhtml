Then Mater looked forward. “Aaaaah!”

A UFO was floating right in front of him!

“Well, hey there,” he said. “Welcome. My name is Mater.”

“My name is Mator,” the UFO replied.

“Mator? Huh.” That sounded an awful lot like his own name,
the tow truck thought. “Should I take you to my liter?”

“Your leader,” the UFO echoed.

Mater rolled off with the UFO flying behind him.
