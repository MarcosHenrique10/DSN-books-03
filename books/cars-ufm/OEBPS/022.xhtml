Outside the tire shop in Radiator Springs, Lightning
 looked skeptical. “I’m sorry, Mater,” he said. “That did
 not happen.”

“Oh, yeah?” Mater replied. “Then how
come I can do  this?”

He tried to fly, but his gears started grinding loudly.

“Oh, Mater, please.” Lightning rolled his eyes and
 drove away.

Finally, Mater rose into the air. With a laugh, he flew
 off into the clear blue sky.
