Then the day of the big rematch arrived—the Florida 500!
Everyone was waiting to see if Storm would take first place
again. Reporters and photographers surrounded him.

“Over here, Storm!” shouted a photographer.

“Give us that championship smile,” said another.

“Lightning McQueen will be racing today,” said a reporter.
“Are you prepared to take him on?”

Storm laughed. “Yeah, I’m not too worried about
the competition.”
