Now it was time to go to Florida. When Lightning
 arrived, he was nervous. He didn’t know if he could
 win. If this was the end of his career, he would be
 heartbroken, like Doc had been.

Smokey said that Doc had loved racing, but he’d
 loved being Lightning’s crew chief even more.

“He saw something in you that you don’t even see
 in yourself,” Smokey told Lightning.
