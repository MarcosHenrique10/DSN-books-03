Soon it was time to race. But
 Lightning thought about Cruz.
 She had trained with him—and
 even beaten him in a race! He knew
 she should be living her dream as
 a racer. She just needed a chance.

Suddenly,
 Lightning had
 an idea.

He would leave the race, and Cruz would finish it!
 Lightning believed in her, just like Doc had
 believed in him. Ramone gave her a cool new paint
 job with the number 95!
